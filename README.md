# NewHuoPoolPathway 
## Overview
The NewHuoPool Allocator focuses on onboarding **Public Open Data** to the Filecoin network.We follow a **manual diligence and staged allocation** process to ensure data compliance, retrievability, and alignment with Filecoin Plus governance requirements.
---
## Allocation Principles
- Manual diligence only 
- First-round allocation capped at **5% / 256 TiB**
- No single client may receive more than **40%** of the total pathway DataCap
- Data must be authentic, verifiable, and retrievable
---
## Storage Requirements
- Minimum **5 Storage Providers**
- Minimum **5 replicas**
- Storage Providers must maintain acceptable retrievability (≥ 75%)
- SP information must be disclosed in advance and remain consistent with on-chain deal records
---
## Risk Control & Compliance
- Verification of data source and ownership
- Monitoring of SP diversity to avoid excessive concentration
- Continuous tracking using tools such as CID Checker and DatacapStats
- The allocator does **not operate, control, or have any financial interest in Storage Providers**
- Allocation decisions are subject to review under the Filecoin Plus governance process
- Non-compliance may result in restricted or suspended allocations
